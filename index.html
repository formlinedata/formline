Ratings are computed in your browser from open match data maintained by Mart Jürisoo (men's and women's results), compiled from Wikipedia, RSSSF and football association sources and released under CC0. Results newer than the archives, and upcoming fixtures, come from API-Football.

Current flags from Flagpedia.net, past flags from Wikimedia Commons, each shown as it was on the date in view; Flags & names has the details and credits. Association and confederation badges, mostly from Logopedia, are the trademarks of their owners, shown for identification; where none is bundled with the site, Wikipedia's image is used. Formline is an independent project, not affiliated with eloratings.net, FIFA or any confederation.
